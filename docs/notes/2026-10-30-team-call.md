## Main idea: underwater fish robot (aquatic rescue)

- Fish-shaped articulated robot with rubber fins and a PVC pipe frame.
- Inspired by the MIT robotic fish.
  Organic movement avoids the snag issues of propellers.
- Neutral buoyancy from lead pellets, and directional control from differential fin thrust.

Hardware Matt already owns:

- 12x 45kg servos and 12x 25kg servos (24 total, waterproof).
- 2x Pololu Maestros (servo control).
- 2x Raspberry Pi (one needed).
- 3x tensor processing units for onboard computer vision.
- Range extender for Raspberry Pi networking.
- 150 degree FOV infrared camera.

Still needed: PVC pipe, rubber sheets, 3D-printed fin arms.

Waterproofing: resin-printed dummy enclosures with leak-test strips, and the camera behind a glass or plastic bubble dome.

## Detection and sensing

- Primary scope narrowed to pools (clear water, no drone needed).
  Drowning is a leading cause of death in children, and apartment complex pools often lack lifeguards.
- Computer vision: detect frantic or thrashing limb movement versus calm swimming.
  Concern: telling drowning apart from floating or playing.
- Ultrasonic distance sensor ($16 waterproof option found) is viable for object detection.
- Sonar was considered for murky or flood water, but it cannot tell a person from debris.
- The robot surfaces to alert.
  Speech underwater is not effective, so flashing lights or buzzing are the alternative signal.
- A drone + robot duo was discussed for beach and flood scope, but deprioritized.
  - Vision confidence at altitude is uncertain, and ocean winds are a concern for small drones.
  - Flood scope adds a software use case (front-end app for emergency responders) but complicates the demo.

## Software and front end

- Pool scope needs only a minimal front end: battery status and an alert notification to a parent's phone.
- AI call agent (like the Code Red crib project) to notify a parent if drowning is detected.
- Privacy: skeleton and pose tracking only, no raw video stored.
- A flood scope would give a richer software case: live robot feed and zone mapping for responders.
- Computer vision pipeline: pose and limb tracking model, similar to the VMR vending machine robot.
