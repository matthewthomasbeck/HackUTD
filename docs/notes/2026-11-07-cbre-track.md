# CBRE sponsor track, 2026-11-07

Sources: a photo of the CBRE track slide, and three IRIS summaries of the team's talk with a CBRE representative named Courtney (screenshots taken 3:15 pm), shared by Cayden on 2026-11-07.
The IRIS text is an AI summary, not a transcript, so check exact wording with whoever was in the conversation.

## Challenge statement (slide)

Build an application that connects fragmented real estate data to predict operational failures, quantify business impact, or execute solutions before disruptions spread.

Prizes: 1st Sony headphones, 2nd Kindle, 3rd digital camera.

## CBRE feedback (IRIS summaries)

- Focus on the problem, the connections, and scalability.
- Target commercial facilities, such as apartment complexes, not individual homes.
- For a robot that detects something like a possible water leak:
  - How does the system verify that the alert is real?
  - How does it securely get useful information to a person, likely through an app?
  - How does it keep that data accurate and up to date?
- Connecting sensors and facility data at scale is a challenge.
- Replacing or changing a sensor must not break the rest of the system.
- The solution should keep working as it scales across many properties.
