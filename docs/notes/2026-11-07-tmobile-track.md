# T-Mobile sponsor track, 2026-11-07

Source: a photo of the T-Mobile challenge slide, shared by Cayden on 2026-11-07.
Status: looked at as a possible third track.
It does not fit the proposed EOG project (see the end of this file).

## Challenge statement (slide)

"Safe Signal": protect customers from scams and fraud in ways T-Mobile has not thought of yet.

You build:

- Any approach: prevention, real-time protection, education, new security features.
- Scam detection is a core piece, not the whole product.
- Go beyond classification: safeguards and customer-facing experiences.
- Clear next steps, honest confidence, privacy by design.

You create the data: build your own synthetic scam messages and scenarios, with no real customer data.

Judges look for creativity, impact, usability, security and privacy, and detection quality.

The big unlock: customers feel protected before, during, and after a threat.

No prizes were shown on the slide.

## Fit with the proposed EOG project

- The T-Mobile challenge is about protecting phone customers from scams and fraud.
  The EOG project is about oilfield water operations, so the two do not share a problem.
- The only overlap is the style of the answer: detection plus a confidence value plus a clear next step.
- A forced link (for example phishing texts aimed at field operators, or spoofed sensor data) would be off topic for both judging panels.
