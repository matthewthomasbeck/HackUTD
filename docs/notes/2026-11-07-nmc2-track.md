## Challenge (slide)

Mission: Keep the World Running.

Behind every AI model, streaming service, and cloud application is a data center, and a team keeping it running around the clock.
A missed warning, an unclear handoff, or the wrong information at the wrong time can turn a small issue into a major disruption.
NMC² challenges teams to build a solution that helps data center teams stay one step ahead.

## Fit with a robot and glasses (not decided)

- The slide names three failures: a missed warning, an unclear handoff, and the wrong information at the wrong time.
- A robot that patrols server aisles with the thermal camera can catch a hot spot that fixed sensors miss (the missed warning).
- Cayden's Even Realities G2 glasses can show a technician the right rack, the problem, and the steps while their hands are busy (the right information at the right time).
- A Nemotron agent can triage alerts, send the robot, and write the shift handoff (the unclear handoff), which also covers the locked NVIDIA track.
- Data center floors are flat, so wheels (the brief's mecanum base) fit better than legs.
  A robot dog adds risk with no clear benefit there.
